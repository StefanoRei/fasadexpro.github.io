extended_text: |
  In early 2025, a shopping center opened in the Rostokino district as part of the BOTANICA multifunctional complex, near the Botanical Garden metro station.
  The multifunctional public and business center unites offices, a shopping and entertainment complex and a public space on a landscaped territory. The area of the facility is about 90 thousand square meters.
  <br><br>
  The shopping and entertainment center, with underground parking and direct access from the Botanical Garden MCC station, occupied four floors, and offices were located from the fourth to the eighteenth floors. The building is distinguished by stylish architectural solutions with a transparent atrium roof, showcase layout of the first floor facades and 'green' design of the internal space.
