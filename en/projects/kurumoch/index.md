Kurumoch International Airport in Samara is the largest and most promising airport in the Volga region, and is among the top ten leading airports in Russia. The construction of a new modern passenger terminal is the first successfully implemented project in preparation for transport infrastructure facilities for the 2018 FIFA World Cup. The facility was built in less than a year and a half. Its area is more than 42 thousand sq.m, capacity - more than 3.5 million people/year. The terminal has 24 check-in counters, 7 jet bridges, 6 exits to apron buses."
