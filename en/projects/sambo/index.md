The total area of the six-story building (5 above-ground floors and underground parking) is more than 40 thousand square meters. The architecture combines monumental concrete forms with dynamic glass facades. The authors of the project are especially proud of the mirrored acoustic ceiling, which used inclined stainless steel panels, and the stained-glass glazing of the facade. This allows people to see athletes training directly from the street."
